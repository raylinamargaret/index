01 · ABOUT THE PLACE

So... what's at USJ? 👀

one day I NEED to go here!

Universal Studios Japan, usually called USJ, is a popular theme park located in Osaka, Japan. It is a place where visitors can enjoy rides, shows, food, shops, and different themed areas. The park is especially interesting because many of its areas are based on movies, games, and famous characters. This makes the experience feel different from visiting an ordinary tourist attraction.

One of the things that makes USJ interesting is the variety of things that visitors can do. Someone who likes exciting rides can spend their time trying different attractions, while someone who prefers taking pictures can explore the colorful buildings and themed areas. There are also restaurants and shops where visitors can try special food and buy souvenirs. Because there are many different things to see, people can choose activities based on what they enjoy.

One of the most recognizable areas in Universal Studios Japan is Super Nintendo World. This area brings the world of Nintendo games into real life. It has bright colors, familiar characters, and decorations that look like they came straight from a video game. Visitors can explore the area and experience attractions inspired by games such as Super Mario. For people who enjoy gaming, this is one of the most exciting parts of the park.

Another popular area is The Wizarding World of Harry Potter. The area is designed to look like the magical world from the Harry Potter stories. The buildings, shops, and streets are carefully designed to create a magical atmosphere. Visitors can also find themed food and drinks while exploring the area. It is a fun example of how a theme park can turn a fictional world into a real place that visitors can walk through.

USJ also has areas based on other movies and characters, including Minion Park. The bright decorations and funny Minion designs make this area feel cheerful and playful. Besides themed areas, visitors can watch performances, eat different kinds of food, and spend time with their friends or family. There are also many places where visitors can take pictures and keep memories from their trip.

I think Universal Studios Japan would be an exciting place to visit because it combines entertainment, technology, creativity, and storytelling. Instead of only looking at something, visitors can actually walk around inside a world based on their favorite movies or games. If I ever get the chance to visit Osaka, USJ would definitely be one of the places I would want to explore.
